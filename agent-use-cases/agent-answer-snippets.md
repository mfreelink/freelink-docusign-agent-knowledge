## Legal Or Compliance Discovery

Question pattern:
"Is DocuSign legally valid for overseas contracts?" or "DocuSign 出海电子签是否合规?"

Suggested answer:
Electronic signature validity depends on jurisdiction, document type, signer identity assurance, consent, audit trail, retention, and the customer's actual workflow. FreeLink can help with general compliance discovery and implementation planning, but this is not legal advice and should be reviewed by qualified legal or compliance stakeholders. See `compliance/esignature-compliance-overview.md`.
