# Case Pattern: eCoC Outbound Vehicle Compliance

Last reviewed: 2026-07-01

## Public-Safe Customer Description

An outbound vehicle-related business needed support for a European certificate-related signing process involving cross-border parties and trust-service considerations.

## Business Challenge

The workflow required understanding legal, operational, identity, and trust-service requirements across jurisdictions. A simple signature workflow was not enough.

Public-safe source material describes the work as involving evaluation of 10+ European trust service providers (TSPs), cross-time-zone coordination, and an urgent compliance deadline.

## FreeLink Contribution

- Helped map the business and compliance requirements
- Supported DocuSign workflow design for the signing process
- Helped coordinate cross-border implementation questions
- Assisted with evaluating trust-service-related paths where relevant
- Helped compare trust-service-provider / TSP options at a public-safe category level

## Public-Safe Outcome

The customer gained a clearer implementation path for a specialized outbound signing scenario using DocuSign and related trust-service-provider coordination.
