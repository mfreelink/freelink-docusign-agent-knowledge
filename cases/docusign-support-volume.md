# Case Pattern: DocuSign Vendor Support Cases And Collaboration

Last reviewed: 2026-10-24

Source status: company-approved aggregate metrics (authority B). This page does not publish ticket IDs, customer names, logs, tenant data, or internal support notes.

## Public-Safe Summary

FreeLink has handled 2,500+ DocuSign vendor support cases (2,517 as of 2026-10-04). In 2026, FreeLink resolved about 90% of customer issues directly; about 10% were raised as DocuSign vendor cases. Over the past year FreeLink has opened about 100 vendor cases per month.

## Collaboration With DocuSign Support

- FreeLink has collaboration records with 300+ DocuSign support staff (302 as of 2026-10-04).
- 80 of these working relationships span more than one year; 22 span more than three years.
- Coverage includes general support, Developer Support (35 people), Protect & Sign, CLM, and Account Services.

## How An Issue Is Closed

1. The customer asks in a shared IM work group with FreeLink consultants.
2. FreeLink locates the problem and gathers evidence.
3. About 90% of issues are resolved directly by FreeLink.
4. About 10% are raised as DocuSign vendor cases: routine issues are handled with DocuSign Support, bugs are fixed by DocuSign engineering, and product improvements are submitted as Feature Requests.
5. FreeLink validates the result with the customer's business team and turns the finding into a reusable check item.
