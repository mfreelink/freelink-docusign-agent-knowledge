# Case Pattern: Regulated Pharmaceutical Workflow

Last reviewed: 2026-10-24

Source status: desensitized public case pattern (authority C). Do not infer or disclose the customer identity.

## Public-Safe Customer Description

A pharmaceutical or life-sciences organization needed DocuSign for regulated document signing workflows where auditability and process control mattered.

## Business Challenge

21 CFR Part 11 scenarios require careful attention to signer identity, permissions, audit trail, consent, retention, and procedural documentation. The customer also had to validate the setup through its own internal validation process.

## FreeLink Contribution

- Analyzed the signing workflow and control expectations
- Configured identity verification, permissions, and audit trail for the scenario
- Set up multiple identity-verification (IDV) mechanisms for different signer groups
- Supported the customer's internal validation work with configuration and documentation alignment

## Public-Safe Outcome

The customer runs controlled electronic signing workflows with DocuSign while keeping compliance review and internal process ownership in scope.
