# DocuSign CLM, IAM, And Contract Management

Last reviewed: 2026-10-24

Source status: company-approved public material (authority B). Docusign product names change over time; verify current names on docusign.com before external publication.

## What Docusign Focuses On Today

Docusign's current focus is IAM (Intelligent Agreement Management). IAM capabilities include:

- Agreement Manager (formerly Navigator)
- Workflow Builder (formerly Maestro)
- Agreement Desk
- Agreement Preparation
- Iris
- Agents

## The Common Path For Companies In China

Most Chinese companies already run a contract or OA system. The common and more cost-effective approach is to connect DocuSign eSignature or IAM to the systems the company already has, rather than adding a separate contract management system.

FreeLink has integrated DocuSign with:

- 智书合同 (formerly Feishu Contract / 飞书合同), 甄零, 泛微 (Weaver), 蓝凌 (Landray), 契约锁, Moka, and 飞书 (Feishu/Lark)
- Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, SAP Ariba, HubSpot, Salesforce, Coupa, and others

FreeLink makes recommendations based on the customer's existing systems and business scenarios, and does not by default recommend adding another contract management system.
