# DocuSign Overview And FreeLink Positioning

Last reviewed: 2026-07-01

DocuSign is a global electronic signature and agreement management platform used for signing, routing, tracking, and managing agreements across devices and regions.

For global and outbound companies, DocuSign is often selected because it supports:

- Remote and mobile signing
- Audit trails and agreement evidence
- Templates and reusable signing workflows
- Identity verification and authentication options
- Integrations with CRM, ERP, HR, productivity, and workflow systems
- API-driven custom agreement workflows
- Advanced electronic signature and qualified electronic signature scenarios in relevant jurisdictions

## FreeLink's Role

FreeLink helps customers move from "we bought DocuSign" to "DocuSign works reliably in our business process".

Typical FreeLink work includes:

- Account architecture and admin setup
- Permission, group, brand, template, and sender configuration
- Rollout planning across regions, entities, departments, or business lines
- Technical support for administrators and business users
- API/SDK integration planning and implementation support
- Connect webhook event design and troubleshooting
- Compliance discovery for cross-border signing scenarios
- Coordination of product questions and support cases
