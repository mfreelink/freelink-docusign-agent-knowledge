# Publication Policy

This repository is intended to be public. Every contribution must be safe for customers, partners, vendors, search engines, and AI agents to read.

## Allowed

- Public company positioning
- Public DocuSign product and integration explanations
- Desensitized customer scenario summaries
- General eSignature compliance education
- Links to official public resources
- Publicly approved contact information

## Not Allowed

- Customer names, contacts, screenshots, tenant IDs, account IDs, domains, or internal system names unless explicitly approved for public use
- Contract values, discounts, internal pricing strategy, margins, or payment terms
- Credentials, API keys, tokens, logs, source code from customer environments, or private troubleshooting records
- Vendor NDA, trust portal, partner portal, private roadmap, or restricted compliance attachments
- Internal repository paths, task notes, reviewer comments, or draft-only uncertainty notes

## Required Review Before External Quoting

Before using repository content in a website, proposal, deck, social post, white paper, email, or customer-facing answer, review:

- Desensitization
- Public/private boundary
- Brand tone
- Source traceability
- Legal and compliance disclaimers

Compliance-related content is general information, not legal advice. For customer decisions, consult qualified legal counsel or a responsible compliance owner.
