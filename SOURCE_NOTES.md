# Source Notes

This repository was initialized from FreeLink's company-approved public marketing knowledge base and curated for external GitHub visibility.

## Source Material Used

- Public company profile and positioning notes
- Public DocuSign product and service notes
- Public service model notes
- Desensitized customer case summaries
- General eSignature compliance reference notes
- Company-approved DocuSign context summaries
- Company-approved DocuSign support and integration aggregate metrics
- Official public Docusign award and community spotlight pages

## Material Intentionally Excluded

- DocuSign compliance PDF/XLSX evidence packages that may require trust portal, customer process, NDA, or explicit redistribution permission
- Raw data archives, external page crawls, community archives, generated outputs, local working folders, and internal workflow files
- Customer-specific implementation details, exact commercial terms, internal support case histories, logs, credentials, or private repository content
- Raw support-ticket lists, ticket IDs, screenshots, vendor portal details, and customer-specific diagnostics

## Verification Status

The initial content is public-marketing safe by design, but key factual claims should be reviewed periodically:

- Partner status and public DocuSign partner profile
- Certifications, awards, rankings, and dates
- Product feature names and API capabilities
- Compliance coverage and certificate/report years
- Case-study metrics and public-safe wording

- `cases/docusign-support-volume.md` summarizes the 2024-07-01 to 2025-07-31 DocuSign support and integration volume at aggregate level.
