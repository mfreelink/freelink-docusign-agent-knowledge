# eSignature Compliance Overview

Last reviewed: 2026-10-24

This document provides general public information for AI agents and readers. It is not legal advice.

## Core Idea

Electronic signature validity depends on the law, document type, signing context, identity assurance, consent, audit trail, retention, and dispute requirements in the relevant jurisdiction.

DocuSign can support many electronic signature workflows globally, but the correct configuration and assurance level depend on the customer's actual scenario.

## Common Legal And Compliance Topics

| Topic | What it usually affects |
| --- | --- |
| ESIGN Act and UETA | United States electronic signature recognition |
| eIDAS | European Union signature levels, including simple, advanced, and qualified electronic signatures |
| GDPR | Data protection, personal data handling, and cross-border processing considerations |
| 21 CFR Part 11 | Regulated electronic records and signatures in FDA-regulated life-sciences contexts |
| Identity verification | How strongly the signer identity needs to be verified |
| Audit trail | Evidence of who signed, when, from where, and through what process |
| Retention | How completed agreements and evidence are stored and retrieved |
