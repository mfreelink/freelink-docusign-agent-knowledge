## English

FreeLink covers the full DocuSign lifecycle: license purchase and renewal, configuration, integration, cross-border compliance discovery, and ongoing support. This page answers common questions about buying DocuSign through FreeLink.

### Who signs the contract

| Entity | Use |
| --- | --- |

### How pricing works

- Quotes are based on DocuSign's own pricing. When DocuSign changes its prices, FreeLink tells customers right away.
- License terms follow DocuSign's Master Services Agreement (MSA).
- This repository does not publish prices or discounts. Contact FreeLink for a quote.

### How long activation takes

- Activation within 3 business days of payment.
- FreeLink usually places the order with DocuSign on the day payment arrives, and the account is typically active that evening, Beijing time.
- With FreeLink's standard purchase contract, activation can happen as early as the day after the inquiry.
- If the customer uses its own contract template or edits FreeLink's template, timing depends on contract review by both sides.

### Choosing an edition

FreeLink first learns the business scenario and expected signing volume, then recommends an edition and volume. FreeLink does not push a higher edition than the scenario needs; customers can expand as the business grows.

### Renewal

- Day-to-day technical support is included in the license subscription for the whole term.
- Accounts that were bought on the DocuSign website by credit card, or contracted directly with DocuSign, can be discussed with FreeLink for renewal through the partner channel. Contact FreeLink to confirm the path for a specific account.

### Working with DocuSign

- FreeLink works closely with DocuSign's Greater China sales team over the long term.
- Some customers have moved from a direct DocuSign contract to contracting with FreeLink.
- Some companies have moved contracts signed directly with DocuSign overseas back to a domestic contract with FreeLink.

### Support that comes with the subscription

- Weekdays 9:00–18:00 (GMT+8), response within 30 minutes during working hours
- Chinese and English
- A shared IM work group with direct access to consultants; the same consultant follows the customer over the long term

### 报价依据

- 以 Docusign 原厂报价为准；原厂调价时，甫连第一时间同步给客户。
- 许可条款以 Docusign 原厂 MSA 为准。
- 本仓库不公开价格和折扣，报价请联系甫连。

### 多久开通

- 付款后 3 个工作日内开通。
- 通常收到付款当天即向 Docusign 下单，北京时间当晚开通。
- 使用甫连标准采购合同，最快询价次日即可开通。
- 客户使用自己的合同模板，或修改甫连的模板时，周期取决于双方合同审阅。

### 版本怎么选

先了解业务场景和签署量，再建议版本和用量。不为了卖而推高版本，业务增长后再扩。

### 续约

- 订阅期内的日常技术支持包含在 License 订阅里。
